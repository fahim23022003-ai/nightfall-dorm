# Original visual-overhaul assets

The illustrated atlas in `art/source_atlas.png` was created specifically for Nightfall Dorm using the available authorized image-generation workflow. No Haunted Dorm image or copyrighted game asset was supplied, extracted or traced. The source atlas and finished crops are stored locally so the game has no external asset/runtime network dependency.

Art direction: cozy-eerie overhead cartoon illustration, dark plum outlines, warm wood/brass, teal bedding and lights, violet midnight moth antagonist. The atlas contains 24 isolated illustrations: two beds; cabinet/fern/lantern/wood door; player idle/two walking poses/sleeping plus two auburn resident poses; five hunter states and reinforced door; six functional machines.

`tools/art/prepare_art.py` extracts the original 6×4 atlas and builds deterministic wood/corridor textures, foundation sigil, window, contact shadow, lantern glow, currency/power symbols and project icon. Pillow is a development-only dependency. The exported game only needs the prepared PNGs. There are 33 runtime art textures/icons plus the retained source atlas. The ICO is the Windows form of the project icon.

`assets/fonts/Display.ttf` and `Interface.ttf` are DejaVu Serif Bold and DejaVu Sans from the environment, distributed under the notices in `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`. Font authorship is not claimed as original game art.

Animation is deliberately lightweight: sprite pose swaps, breathing/walk bob, ghost attack/recoil/dissolve states, turret aim motion/traces, impact sparks and upgrade/income feedback. It is not a fully rigged or four-direction animation set. Two survivor base designs and palette variants are used; every bot does not yet have a unique illustrated body/sleep sheet.

## Phase 4B Grim Reaper

`boss/` contains four original authored SVG robe animation frames and a separate scythe. `tools/art/generate_reaper.py` is the complete reproducible source, with no downloaded or traced assets. Layered hood, skull, red eyes, mantle folds, chain/clasp, skeletal hands and ragged hems are distinct from the original Hunter. `scripts/boss_presentation.gd` animates frame swaps, cloth breathing, hover, articulated scythe wind-up/sweep, deterministic smoke curls, charge rings, silence marks and hurt/death reactions. These are lightweight original 2D vector illustrations; no claim of fully rigged/high-frame hand-painted animation is made.

## Phase 5A Mythic badge

`ranks/mythic.svg` is an original authored purple/gold crown-and-crystal prestige badge extending the six existing rank badges. The runtime animates its color and promotion scale without blocking result actions. No external imagery or new gameplay perk is attached to Mythic.

## Phase 5B-2B Legendary art

## Phase 5C-2 haunted scenery and prestige defenses

A read-only procedural environment adds five themed exterior sets, bounded ambient
silhouettes/eyes/crawlers/watchers/apparitions and weather over existing geometry.
`tools/art/generate_prestige_defenses.py` authors twelve original SVG armor tiers
for Needle/Arc Levels 5–10, layered with existing sprites, fins/crown/badges and
bounded projectiles. Actual combat events drive fear, strike, collapse and corpse
presentation. No external assets, playable map or extra combat enemy was added.


## Phase 5C-3 Gothic industrial presentation

`assets/visual_identity/` contains six original authored SVGs: a cracked Gothic
menu facade and five map-specific floor surfaces (Dorm, Hospital, Hotel, Lab,
Mansion). `tools/art/generate_visual_identity.py` reproduces their vector geometry;
no external image was downloaded, traced or copied. `GothicTheme` creates cached
256×128 original material textures at runtime, with weathered bevels, restrained
grain, rivets, edge hatching and glass detail. These shared nine-slice surfaces
replace flat panel/button fills without a runtime network or extra dependency.

Local room/corridor props, door scratches, breach debris, scorch marks and Hunter
motion use bounded procedural drawing alongside existing original artwork. The
existing separately illustrated Reaper robe/scythe and Level 10 armor are reused.
The licensed DejaVu Serif Bold display font is reused for the wordmark; existing
DejaVu Sans remains the body font. Existing font attribution still applies.
This is a layered 2D treatment, not a new fully rigged character animation set;
retained survivor/bed sprites and simple exterior silhouettes remain art limits.


## Phase 5D-1 premium UI and strategy

`tools/art/generate_premium_ui.py` authors30 original action/currency/mode/
archetype/branch SVG emblems in `assets/premium_ui/`, plus four improved Common
supply illustrations at the existing repair/gold/battery/radar item paths.
The geometric silhouettes, gradient materials, shadows and engraved details
are authored locally, with no copied stock/game imagery. Existing UI material
textures gain restrained dimensional button rails, pressed depth and focus edges.
Archetype/branch overlays reuse existing original actors and prestige armor.

`assets/fonts/GrenzeGotisch-SemiBold.ttf` is a locally bundled static600-weight
instance of Grenze Gotisch from Google Fonts/Omnibus-Type, produced with
fontTools. `GrenzeGotisch-OFL.txt` includes SIL OFL1.1 and author copyright;
`GrenzeGotisch-SOURCE.txt` records the exact source. This licensed typeface is
not claimed as original game art. Heading/title/mode/event use is separate from
the retained licensed DejaVu Sans body font. No machine-installed font or runtime
asset download is required. All95 printable English ASCII glyphs were inspected;
actual typography and icons are independently checked through Godot rendering.


## Phase 5D-2 replay identity and cosmetics

`tools/art/generate_replay_art.py` authors nine original SVGs in
`assets/premium_ui/`: Endless skull, challenge, practice, cosmetics, analysis,
and four appearance previews. Original runtime drawing in
`scripts/cosmetic_presentation.gd` adds arched/riveted door ironwork, turret
spires/halo/casing, bed posts/canopy and the scythe HUD crest. These are shape/
detail changes layered with existing damage, specialization and prestige art;
no external imagery, paid asset or runtime download was introduced. Existing
local OFL/DejaVu font licenses remain unchanged. Cosmetic data has no combat or
economy multiplier and is read only by presentation. Actual rendered inspection
and its quality limits are recorded in the Phase5D2 report/evidence.
