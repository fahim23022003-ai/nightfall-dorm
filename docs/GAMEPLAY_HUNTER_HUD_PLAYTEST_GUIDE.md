# Gameplay overhaul — Windows playtest guide

For a Windows test archive, extract all files and launch `NightfallDorm.exe`.
For source, use the latest `work` branch, open `project.godot` in Godot4.6.3 and pressF5.
Version0.7.0-overhaul identifies this update. Keep your existing profile; Practice
uses separate temporary resources and inventory. Native Windows and human sound,
visual quality and usability remain unverified by the Linux cloud tests.

1. Start an ordinary campaign match. With menus closed, check that Build, Bed,
   Door, selected Upgrade and Repair stay along the bottom and the center is clear.
   Select a pad: the illustrated drawer should slide up in about a quarter second.
   Close it or press Escape; it should slide down. Taking damage should not close it.
2. Buy a Needle once, rapidly double-click a purchase/upgrade, then cancel a held
   purchase with Escape. Check exactly one successful payment and no stuck state.
   Inspect current/next stats, an exclusive specialization and the sale quote.
   Selling should return half of actual separate Gold/Electricity investment.
3. Start Endless. Your fresh room has3 positions. Purchase expansion after meeting
   the displayed Bed/Door and resource requirements:6,8,10,11,12,14,16. Watch the
   floor and back wall grow while your door, bed and existing machines stay fixed.
   Place a building on a newly unlocked pad. Repeat on the horizontal Lab layout.
4. End an Endless wave at a genuine checkpoint and resume through the existing
   menu. Confirm resources, expansion, actual investment, levels and branches.
   Older saved rooms should retain their historical buildings and positions.
5. In Practice, try Phantom Witch, Electric Ghost and Storm Wraith. Watch warnings,
   projectile travel, temporary interference and legal chain attacks. Physical and
   Magic hit feedback should differ. Four small affinity icons have concise tips;
   favorable counters are modest bonuses, never immunity.
6. Try Barrier, Plasma and Antenna against Magic. Barrier returns damage but does
   not cancel the original hit. Plasma stores temporary energy and spends it on
   bounded door repair. Antenna uses Electricity to replenish finite magic shielding.
   Physical strikes bypass these effects. Compare their actual Level5 branch
   previews and Level10 visuals. Multiple copies do not stack freely: the
   highest-level device applies; equal levels use the first placed device.
7. Check all Hunter silhouettes and actual walking, strike, hurt, defensive,
   special and defeat poses. Reaper should preserve the existing transformation,
   both phases, undead and Crimson encounter while adding Soul Drain and a
   warned legal Shadow Dash. Watch a nearby ranged Hunter while your drawer is
   closed/open; the camera should keep it visible without changing just for menus.
8. Listen for distinct warnings, Magic impacts, reflection, shield and energy
   release. Confirm your saved Music/SFX settings and Reduced Effects still work.
   Cloud PCM checks establish signal and clipping bounds; they do not establish
   how frightening or satisfying the sound feels on your headphones.
9. Check teammates, spectating/Home, own-door danger alerts, twenty consumables,
   ranks/rewards, shop, spin/pity and cosmetics. Practice should spend no real
   inventory and grant no permanent rewards.
10. Play active and passive Hell strategies. Electric Ghost on Underground Lab is
    a demanding tested case; record map, stage, Hunter, equipment, builds and
    decisions when reporting balance. Automated seeds do not establish a universal
    win rate. Challenge ruleset4 codes normalize equipment; old codes are retained
    as archives rather than silently played with changed rules. Manual results
    remain self-reported, with no server leaderboard.

For each issue, record version, mode/map/Hunter, window resolution, exact action,
expected result and observed result. A screenshot or short video helps identify
visual/input problems. Specifically assess whether the new bottom workflow is
clear to a first-time player and whether art and sound feel strong enough during
real combat; those judgments require the owner and Adie.
