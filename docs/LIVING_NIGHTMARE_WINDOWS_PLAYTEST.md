# Nightfall Dorm Living Nightmare Windows preview

Extract the entire archive into an empty folder. Open **Play Living Nightmare
Preview.cmd**. It starts a separate profile and enables the Living Nightmare
work alongside Phase 14F visuals, Full Room Grid and Procedural Maps V2.
The V2.3, V1 and fixed reference launchers are available for comparison.

- Open **Continue Story**, choose a difficulty track, review the mission and
  loadout, then start. Complete a mission and confirm the result names the next
  level. Lose after advancing and check the displayed checkpoint and setback.
- Switch difficulties from the Story screen and check that each track retains
  its own position. Rank and inventory are shared. Open **Endless Mode**
  separately from the main menu.
- Listen while moving from menu to preparation and then to the first siege.
  Watch a teammate's siege first, then your own, and check that the short
  heartbeat plays only for a new siege on your door. Try the music settings.
- Observe the Hunter at room scale on both Standard and Low graphics. Record
  the Hunter type, direction, level, attack and any concerns. Note the map
  and room seed when reporting a gameplay issue.
- Check a Haunted Hall recovery, an ordinary victory and a Reaper encounter.
  Include resolution, graphics setting, GPU and frame rate in your notes.

The pack verification is performed with Linux Godot. Native Windows
operation and Intel UHD performance are separate owner playtest checks.
