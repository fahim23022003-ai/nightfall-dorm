# Phase 5D-2 independent balance audit

Agent A; Godot 4.6.3 Linux; 12 November 2026. Production changes were made by B.
A changed only its new harness/evidence, made no commits or pushes, and did not
run the root-owned regular/legacy extended suites.

## Final scope and honest interpretation

Final-source evidence comprises **39 bounded natural policy runs and 1295
completed waves**: 24 matched early/mid runs, 12 advanced runs, two canonical
earned-checkpoint runs and one full-layout readiness crosscheck. These are
deterministic diagnostic policies, not independent human samples or win rates.
No universal fun, fairness, balance or longest-survival guarantee is claimed.

All live runs start from the actual configured map spawn and walk through legal
navigation before physical room entry, claim and sleep. Actual time, income,
Dynamos, paid building/branch transactions, 50% paid-investment refunds and
combat determine progression. No live HP, funds, electricity, positions, clocks,
wave indices or free items are injected. The only between-wave choice is the
real one-time **10% intact-door repair**. No 25 Gold/5 electricity choices are
used. Intermission waiting is not used to earn income. Current campaign prices
are unchanged.

Prepared progression and inventory are disclosed test budgets, not naturally
earned profile stock:

- Main/Normal advanced runs: Master, Dreamer + Last Light, four Shields and one
  Guardian Heart; two item slots, one Legendary for the entire run.
- Separate advanced Long Night/Hell arms: Mythic, Steady Aim + Reaper Slayer,
  four Frost Bombs and one Time Freeze; two slots, one Legendary for the run.
- The separate controlled damage fixtures use one actual Cursed Fortune and no
  perks. They are not natural reachability evidence.

The `typical` script label means a fixed **one-second decision policy**, not a
measured typical human. `optimized` means a fixed **0.2-second decision policy**.
Both follow explicit support/economy/weapon plans; the optimized plan favors
earlier economy. Active late arms may repair repeatedly at this fast rate.
Passive arms stop buying, manually repairing and activating items once their
freeze condition is reached; automatic stations/relays and the modest
intermission repair remain legal.

## Matched early/mid matrix

The 24 current-source cases pair 12 identical map/difficulty/seed/trait/branch
configurations across the two policies. They cover all five maps, all three
difficulties, all three automatically rotating archetypes, all three room traits
and all four weapon branches. This bounded matrix is not the full Cartesian
product; room/archetype strength cannot be ranked independently of geometry.

All **24 complete 12 waves**, for 288 completed waves and 48 genuine milestone
Reaper encounters. All 24 reach Level 10; **16 have actual Level 10 impacts**.
The eight zero-impact cases establish paid availability only: teammate attacks
and Hunter targeting can decide the result elsewhere. Every branch has actual
Level 10 impact cases (Precision 2/6, Suppression 6/6, Focus 4/6, Storm 4/6).
The optimized policy finishes faster in seven pairs, slower in one, with four
ties. This does not establish a universally dominant policy.

The within-wave growth cap materially changes earlier evidence: two optimized
Hell openings that previously lost now complete 12 waves. Consequently the
entire bounded 24-case matrix was refreshed once; prior-source rows and logs are
retained as `balance-results-before-growth.json` and
`balance-matrix-final-before-growth.log`. Final rows are `balance-results.json`
and `balance-matrix-final.log`.

## Passive plateau, tuning and active counterplay

Initial 18.7× HP / 2.475× damage / 1.25× speed caps allowed earned Hospital
Needle/Arc builds to coast through 70 waves without combat actions after wave
20. A reported this to B/root. A 50× / 6× / 55%-contact-repair candidate broke
some smaller layouts but still let 16-pad Mansion builds coast through 75.
Both candidate data sets remain preserved; neither is presented as final balance.

B's final Endless-only rules preserve the first 20 wave factors, then increase
HP/damage to **100× / 9×**, keep speed capped at 1.25× and reduce contact repair
to 35% by wave 60, composed with difficulty repair pressure. HP reaches its cap
at wave **124**, damage at **83**. Within-wave growth is capped at one escalation
step/level 2. Reaper waves remain multiples of five, with additional multiples of
three from wave 30. Existing attack windups/recoveries remain active.

| Current-source fixed plan | Passive outcome | Matched active outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospital Economy, two Storm Arc 10 / two Relay 4 / two Repair 4 | Defeat wave 47 | Completes 75; late trough 23.8 / 1800 HP |
| Mansion Fortified, **full** four Focus Arc 10 / four Relay 4 / four Repair 4 | Fully built wave 24 / 598.1 s, then no actions; defeat wave 77 | Completes 130; waves 60+ trough 1095.9 / 2070 HP |
| Mansion Power, full four Storm Arc 10 / four Relay 4 / four Repair 4 | No actions from 20; defeat wave 77 | Completes 130; waves 60+ trough 592.3 / 1620 HP |

Each fortress also has two Dynamo 4 and two amplifier 4, filling all 16 pads.
The original Fortified freeze-at-20 arm had not finished construction; it is
explicitly superseded for **max-layout** claims by
`balance-max-layout-results.json`. The readiness crosscheck freezes only after
all four weapons reach 10 and all required support stations reach 4. Its
unchanged fully active counterpart is the 130-wave advanced arm.

The full Fortified passive arm's final wave 77 records 11 Hunter hits, 2341.6
observed cumulative door damage, zero manual repairs and 22498.4 Hunter HP
remaining. It still holds 210719.6 Gold / 26891.1 electricity when eliminated:
money alone does not protect an unattended door. Other passive arms fail at
wave 41 (Hospital Needle) and 53 (Dorm Arc). Dorm's selected room has 12 pads.
The gun-heavy Mansion freeze-at-20 arm fails at 75 but still has one Level 8 Arc;
it is **not** claimed as a fully maxed eight-Arc passive test.

The active 130-wave Fortified arm finishes with 357055.9 Gold / 49719.9 power;
its wave 128 has 24 observed player hits and 130 paid repairs. The Power arm
finishes with 612674.1 Gold / 91083.1 power; wave 128 has 25 hits and 151 paid
repairs. In wave 125 the Power arm experiences eight Scythes, three Sieges and
two Surges, with a 658.2-HP trough and 49 paid repairs. These are actual attacks
and paid responses, not just invariant counts.

Separate finite Mythic/Frost/Freeze active fortress arms complete wave 75 in
Long Night and Hell, with late troughs 774.0 and 274.3 HP respectively. Each uses
exactly four Frost and one Freeze for the entire run; no replenishment occurs.
This demonstrates bounded active viability, not that humans can reproduce the
same rapid repair cadence or that every build can survive to a requested wave.

Some passive runs break their door in a cleared wave and are eliminated during
the next breach approach. A zero door-hit count in that following wave is not
an instant unexplained strike. Door HP and the inability to repair a destroyed
door carry through intermission. Presentation-event counters are observed
records; a wave's cleanup can remove a last-frame event. Hunter's own attack
counter and actual HP/resource state are also recorded.

## Actual bounded damage composition, separately controlled

Two explicitly controlled high-wave fixtures set wave/time/position/wallet,
perform real paid door upgrades, consume one actual Cursed Fortune and run the
real attack controller. They are **not natural reachability evidence**.

- Hell Crusher, growth 1, damage factor 9, Curse, charged attack:
  `(22 + 18) × 1.5 × 9 × 1.2 × 1.25 × 1.9 = 1539` observed damage. A full
  max-level Power door goes from 1620 to **81 HP**. Actual Crusher 0.65 s and
  charged 0.9 s warnings are recorded.
- Transformed Crusher Soul Siege substitutes the bounded Reaper damage factor
  1.07 and 1.6 special: **1155.6** observed damage, leaving **464.4 HP**, with
  its actual 1.8 s warning.

Thus the bound is measured through the source composition, not an illustrative
320-damage estimate. It does not protect an already damaged door or make late
Hell forgiving. `--damage-bound` / `--check` runs these two small regression
fixtures and writes only `balance-damage-bound-results.json`.

## Stress observations, pity and provenance

Final advanced peaks are 15 active projectiles, 25 combat-event records and 128
presentation records; each cleared-wave checkpoint has zero active projectiles.
The two 130-wave runs keep the engine-object monitor at 1511 through waves
60–130. Observed static-memory growth over that interval is about 241 KB, which
includes the audit's intentionally retained per-wave result dictionaries; this
is not an isolated production allocation measurement. Their final saved
profiles are about 18 KB. No overflow, unbounded entity/event accumulation,
wallet/stock drift or repeated Legendary activation was observed in this scope.
No hardware FPS or infinite-session memory guarantee is inferred.

The separate `pity-balance-summary.md` documents 500,000 seeded registered-table
draws. Current 8/40 gives observed Legendary 5.534%, mean gap 18.070 and maximum
40; the Epic+ guarantee renormalizes to exactly 80% Epic / 20% Legendary.
Threshold alternatives and a declared quick-loss pacing comparison support
retaining 8/40. Ineligible quick losses grant no spin/progress; Endless waves do
not generate campaign Victory pity draws. No paid gambling is introduced.

`balance-source-manifest.json` records the baseline HEAD, engine, current source
SHA256 values, artifact hashes and explicit output provenance. Final source
evidence is the refreshed 24-case matrix, 12 advanced arms, one full-layout
crosscheck, two earned exports and two controlled damage fixtures. All exited
0 with zero harness integrity/settlement failures and clean Godot error logs.
Defeats are gameplay outcomes, not harness failures. Initial helper-policy
mistakes and previous tuning candidates remain separately labelled evidence.
