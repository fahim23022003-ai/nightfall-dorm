# Phase 5D-4 baseline render review

Baseline source: `d507600f64c3c55ff858a9d5bc5d3c938b6e5f6d` on `work`.

TESTED: A fresh real Godot 4.6.3 Linux/Xorg/Mesa render completed with **53 checks, 0 failures, exit 0**, producing **22 new PNGs** at 1280×720 and 1920×1080. Actual mouse/key input opened purchase cards, previewed and confirmed a paid Dynamo purchase, purchased its Level 2 upgrade, and purchased an existing Needle specialization. The log has no script/parse errors; its sole warning is unsupported V-Sync switching on the dummy display. `render-manifest.json` binds image and baseline-source hashes. Production files were unchanged immediately after capture. The render gate completed; production edits remain held separately until the full baseline regression gate finishes.

The fixture uses an isolated Practice profile with the actual temporary 20,000 Gold/5,000 Electricity budget and real paid existing defenses. Teammate ownership/alive state and the Hunter's attack pose are controlled for layout inspection. The tutorial hint is deliberately visible. Gameplay simulation is held between render steps. These frames establish UI and input behavior, not natural economy, enemy difficulty or human play quality.

## Concrete findings

1. **Opening build UI shifts the world.** At 720p, opening an empty pad moved camera x from 325.00 to 465.58 world units, with the same y, zoom (1.25641) and player position (43, −17). At 1080p the same change was +140.62. This visibly relocates the room and cursor relationship even though the character does not move. See `01-gameplay-hud-*` versus `03-purchase-cards-*` and the measured observations JSON.
2. **Context controls relocate between actions.** The baseline right panel is always x=900 in logical 1280×720 coordinates but changes its top and height: build y246/h382, confirmation y356/h272, machine upgrade y328/h300, specialization y284/h344, bed y378/h250, door y328/h300. Build and door geometry are also directly measured in JSON; other positions are visible in the PNGs and confirmed by baseline `scripts/interface.gd:447–450`. The requested consistent bottom panel would remove this moving target.
3. **Persistent HUD competes with interaction.** Resource/time/Hunter information spans the top; a second central door alert and right teammate strip sit below it. Tutorial/help occupies the left, consumables the lower left, and bed/door/build/repair controls fill the bottom. On 720p the build and branch panels consume much of the right room view. This is a density/hierarchy concern rather than a missing-input defect; the warning, resource figures and tested controls are readable in these fixtures.
4. **Upgrade comparisons are incomplete.** The Dynamo inspector shows current Level 1, base 1.5 Electricity/s and the Level 2 price, but does not show the resulting 3.0 Electricity/s before purchase or distinguish base from effective room-adjusted production. Bed and door panels do provide a simple next-income/HP line. A consistent current → next table would improve decision-making across types.
5. **Specializations are functional, but choice presentation lacks comparison and confirmation.** The existing Needle modal has distinct icons, cost and real damage/cadence/range/slow tradeoff descriptions. It does not compare the selected building's exact current and resulting numbers side by side. Clicking a branch immediately spends and applies the exclusive Level 5 choice. The screenshots do not support calling the branches cosmetic-only; the actual branch effect is visible in the post-purchase inspector.
6. **Purchase cards communicate identity and price but little depth.** Six illustrated cards show names, Gold/Electricity and a short role phrase. There is no expanded selected-card explanation/stat preview in the baseline grid. The actual placement-confirmation screen does clearly state the locked pad, resource cost, Confirm and no-charge Cancel.

All 22 frames were reviewed together; full-size 720p gameplay, Needle branches and Dynamo inspector, plus 1080p purchase cards were inspected independently. No cached evidence was used. These observations support the owner's requested interaction and readability changes; 53 passing input assertions do not establish that the current UX is satisfying.
