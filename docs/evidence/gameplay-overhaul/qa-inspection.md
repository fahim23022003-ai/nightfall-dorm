## Mandatory independent test plan

### Room expansion and geometry

1. **Exact fresh progression:** in real newly begun Endless, assert 3 → 6 → 8 → 10 → 11 → 12 and the documented finite paid steps to 16. Verify each displayed cost, actual debit, increasing valid costs, maximum rejection and unchanged campaign/Practice capacities where expansion is Endless-only.
2. **Authoritative ownership:** reject expansion without a claimed occupied living room, on someone else's room, after elimination/terminal, while paused, and in unsupported modes. Reject invalid tiers and insufficient Gold or Electricity without partial state changes.
3. **Transaction/cancel:** actual mouse purchase plus rapid double-click, keyboard repeat, held-click/Escape and stale panel activation charge once or nothing as appropriate. Test coupon retained/consumed according to the displayed policy, failed persistent save rollback, and same checkpoint atomicity for currency and tier.
4. **Stable geometry matrix:** all five maps × six room orientations/indices × every tier. For every existing pad, door, bed and building assert the same world position before/after. Bounds/visible area must genuinely grow. Every newly unlocked building footprint fits inside usable walls, avoids door/bed/furniture/access lanes and all neighbours/corridors. Test the last allowed and first forbidden pad using both direct API and real world clicks.
5. **Real navigation:** actual Hunter and undead approach/breach, player return-to-bed route, teammate path and decoy navigation before/after expansion, including cached routes created before purchase. No teleports, blocked-room entry, stale path through walls or unbounded graph/cache growth.
6. **Camera and selection:** own/focused/full-map and teammate spectator framing show the expanded room with valid finite zoom on all maps. Expansion/UI opening cannot move the player, change ownership or start a build on a neighbour. Repeated growth must not duplicate pad IDs.
7. **Natural Endless:** run a legitimate earning policy through paid expansion and actual use of newly unlocked slots, survive a wave, checkpoint, reload and continue. Keep this evidence distinct from injected-budget geometry fixtures.

### Typed combat and the three new defenses

8. **Documented attack matrix:** ordinary claws, charged Crusher, Ravager combo, original three specializations, Reaper Scythe Slash, Soul Drain, Shadow Dash, old scythe/siege/surge, Phantom Scream, embedded scythe, undead, Needle/Arc, Flame/Frost/Shock, Inferno and explosive trap. Assert class and separate affinity on actual launch/impact/door feedback, and real numeric damage through the same rules.
9. **Independent numeric order:** calculate expected Physical/Magic hits from explicit fixtures before/after armor, affinity, finite barrier, Shield/Barricade, Guardian/Feather, Reaper resistance, stage/perk and evolution factors. Apply each exactly once. Reject NaN/negative/unknown type values according to the documented API; previews cannot alter combat state.
10. **Barrier reflection:** a true eligible Magic hit produces bounded reflection against the actual living attacker and starts its cooldown. Physical and ineligible packets do not reflect. Multi-barrier and simultaneous hits respect aggregate caps/refractory. Reflected packets cannot recursively reflect; zero-block/zero-damage cases do not create damage or rewards. Test boss/undead death, stale identity, domain foreign-room isolation, attacker disappearing mid-flight and reflection during phase guards.
11. **Plasma storage:** actual Magic absorption fills only to its finite temporary cap; Physical cannot fill it. Test partial capacity, overflow, expiry, spending its stated defensive use once, no resource underflow, no refund inclusion and no conversion to permanent Electricity/inventory. Sell/destruction/wave transitions/resume must implement the displayed retention/cleanup policy without duplication.
12. **Antenna shield:** actual Magic durability decreases, damage exceeding remaining durability reaches the door correctly, Physical is unaffected according to its role, regeneration obeys finite cooldown/cost and cannot stack into indefinite immunity. Test several Antennas plus Shield/Barrier/Plasma and existing defensive items.
13. **Economy for all new machines:** legitimate placement, insufficient-resource failures, every supported level/branch, maximum rejection, coupon at base/upgrade/branch where eligible, actual per-operation spending and separate 50% investment refund. Repeated sale, same-pad rebuild, failed payment/save, destruction and malformed checkpoint must not produce free stock/currency/energy/cooldown resets.
14. **Existing consumables:** all 20 stock transactions and failed activation remain valid with new classes/defenses. In particular Inferno, trap, Reaper Seal, Time Freeze, Guardian, Feather and temporary item locks must use the actual victim/attacker and preserve one-Legendary restriction.
