# V2.3 paired Campaign outcomes

Godot 4.6.3 ran 305 paired headless matches to a terminal result with the same geometry, seed, room and opening wallet in both arms. The 270 broad pairs crossed ten map families, three seeds per family and difficulty, and three plans. Another 35 targeted pairs tested alternative Hell openings. The broad harness claimed the room and started normal sleep from the bed. Seven separate walk pairs included the opening travel. All cases passed the harness's movement, wallet and Hall checks. All 90 Standard broad pairs matched field for field. [Match harness](../../../tools/nightmare_tactics/check_starting_rooms.gd) · [V2.2 comparison](../nightmare-maps-v22/qa/README.md)

A direct reversal is a defense-induced `A → B → A` with no third-room approach or encounter, breach, resident elimination or Hall retreat in between. The cohort showed no replacement three-room cycle of consecutive defense reroutes. Other Hunter reassessments remained available. All 35 additional targeted results retained their prior outcome. The Labyrinth Room 6 early-bed route was the single targeted trace that changed. It stopped returning from Room 6 to Room 2, routed to Room 1 at 107.25 seconds, and still ended in victory. Five NPCs died compared with three in V2.2.

### Four Hard passive wins and the changed active outcomes

All four previous Hard passive victories dealt zero player damage. The two repeat-loop cases changed to defeats. In Hybrid 12077 Room 1, the Hunter did not return from NPC Room 3 to NPC Room 4 at 145.35 seconds, then broke Room 3 at 163.50 seconds. NPC damage to the Hunter fell from 6,615 to 1,998. As the Hunter held its route, three rooms survived instead of five. In Ring 60899 Room 2, it stayed at NPC Room 5 when it previously sent itself back to NPC Room 6 at 134.40 seconds. It breached Room 5 at 165.75 seconds and eliminated its resident at 168.90 seconds. NPC damage fell from 9,140 to 5,064 and three rooms survived instead of all six. The Scattered 5076 Room 6 and Scattered 62900 Room 4 wins matched field for field. Each contained one real NPC defense-induced reroute, with NPC damage of 6,091 and 7,515 respectively.

Both active changed outcomes came from Hell Ring 61167 Room 5. After switching from NPC Room 6 to NPC Room 3, the Hunter redirected to Room 2 at 108.75 seconds instead of returning to Room 6. Room 6 survived both new runs, and the first player-door strike came at 261.60 seconds instead of 347.25. Player damage to the Hunter rose from 2,762 to 4,387 with Balanced and from 2,007 to 4,089 with Needle-first. NPC damage rose from 4,036 to 4,445 and 4,739 respectively. Both runs changed from defeat to victory. The same anti-reversal behavior can strengthen or weaken the Hunter's position depending on later exposure and defender investment.

With six active trials per family and difficulty, the only family-level win-count change was Hell Ring, from 2/6 to 4/6. Hell Branching, Courtyards and Labyrinth remained 0/6 on the two broad plans. The targeted Labyrinth tests below used more openings and room choices. The broad counts describe these fixtures, and the sample cannot establish general win rates or viability of every room on every seed.

## Targeted Hell openings

A separate movement replay claimed Labyrinth 10358 Room 2 after 2.40 seconds of travel, then reached the bed and slept at 3.00 seconds. All seven walk-case rows matched across V2.2 and V2.3. Early bed and early crossfire still won with the usual 25 Gold, zero Electricity and no items. Early fortification lost after the walk. The earned-control run placed its Frost Tower at 6.75 seconds, logged 51 control hits and also won. This puts two reproducible item-free openings within the actual travel budget.

The Labyrinth 10358 sweep tested all six rooms. Room 2 had three item-free wins, using early bed investment, early fortification and approach-aligned Needle crossfire. The earned-control run also won. Balanced, Needle-first and early Dynamo lost in Room 2. Room 3 lost all four tested plans, while Room 5 won all four. Rooms 1, 4 and 6 also had item-free wins with at least one opening. Room 2 received its first hit at 48.0 seconds in all six item-free openings. The successful bed opening paid 30 and 85 Gold for its first two bed upgrades by 28.05 seconds. The fortification opening paid for two door upgrades by 43.35 seconds. The crossfire opening placed four approach-aligned Needles by 27.15 seconds. Different plans can share later purchases once they enter the same combat state.

Hybrid 12336 illustrated a different trade-off. Room 3 won with early bed, early Dynamo and early fortification, and with earned control. Survival Transition still lost. Room 4 won with Survival Transition. The other four plans tested there lost. These tests started with the usual 25 Gold and 0 Electricity. The earned-control cases spent limited items earned through a separate warm-up victory and profile shop purchase. In Labyrinth Room 2, that loadout paid for an early Frost Tower at 3.75 seconds. The item-free cases used neither inventory items nor injected resources.

All three historical reversals were caused by a single defense-induced change of target in V2.2. Labyrinth redirected from NPC Room 2 to another NPC in Room 6 at 63 seconds. Room 6 fell earlier and Room 2 fell later, changing both player starts' subsequent fights. The Hunter's cumulative attack-state time at the player door fell from 53.70 to 23.55 seconds in Room 1 and from 47.40 to 17.25 seconds in Room 4. In Hybrid, a switch from NPC Room 4 to another NPC in Room 5 at 154.65 seconds changed the later order and cut Room 3's attack-state time from 21.00 to 14.40 seconds. These changes delayed the first strike on the player in all three starts, then reduced the time available to stop that breach. Preserving the prior defense in the next route comparison does not change these first reroutes. Different paid openings provided real counterplay in the same rooms.
