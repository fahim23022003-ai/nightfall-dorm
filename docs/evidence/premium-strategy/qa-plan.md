## Acceptance approach

Prepared model/profile fixtures establish accounting and combat invariants;
their injected resources/HP/time are never reported as natural balance evidence.

- Archetypes: reproducible choice, immutable after start, actual movement and
  wind-up/recovery tradeoffs; all five maps ×three difficulties ×three identities
  retain valid floor/contact/breach/elimination and one actual Hunter. Reaper
  transforms once and keeps bounded modifiers and functional special attacks.
- Specializations: milestone prerequisite and mutual exclusion; real projectile
  damage/rate/range/slow behavior with finite bounded control. Level10 maximum.
  No airborne or idle-shot banking exploit. Overdrive/control/Reaper interactions.
- Accounting: invalid branch, insufficient Gold/Power, duplicate choice, failed
  coupon save and terminal/paused actions are atomic. Discounted purchase/upgrade/
  branch payments form actual separate ledgers. Sale returns exactly half actual
  eligible investment, once; tile reuses safely; repeated buy/sell cannot gain.
- Room traits: actual initial/maximum door, occupied income and generator output
  tradeoffs compose once with permanent perks. Six rooms and valid geometry stay;
  bot residents can claim, occupy and buy normally. Tutorial isolation remains.
- Contracts: opt-out and eligibility, real irreversible events (including turrets
  later sold), genuine terminal result binding, no abort/defeat/invalid/duplicate
  payout. First-clear ledger survives reload; failed saves roll back cash, XP,
  items, stage, achievements, match receipt and reward RNG, then retry exactly once.
- Persistence: old progressed saves preserve all20 items, rank/perks, campaign,
  statistics, cash, shop tokens, reward queues/settings/tutorial. No invented
  mid-match save guarantee; project has transient in-match building dictionaries.
- UI: real scene controls, button activation/keyboard states, archetype/branch/
  room/contract descriptions, actual card/viewport rectangles at720p/1080p.
  D inspects real paired renders; test metadata is not visual-quality proof.

Full regular/extended results belong to root. Native Windows execution, physical
keyboard/mouse/speakers, target hardware FPS and subjective aesthetics are
UNVERIFIED unless actually exercised separately. Passing automation does not
prove universal balance or human enjoyment.
