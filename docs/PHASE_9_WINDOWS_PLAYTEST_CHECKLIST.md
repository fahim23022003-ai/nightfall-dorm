# Phase 9 — Windows manual playtest

Native Windows and human comfort are UNVERIFIED in the cloud evidence.
Download the **work** branch ZIP, extract it, and import `project.godot` with
Godot4.6.3. Keep a backup of your existing profile before testing a new build.
Start Endless; Campaign/Practice intentionally keep their previous pad system.

- At1280×720 and1920×1080, choose a room, sleep, and open Build at the bottom/B.
- Choose Needle: only its preview appears; Gold must not change yet.
- Move around unlocked floor; click green to buy once. Try rapid double-clicking.
- Try the wall, bed, cabinet, plant, door route and another structure: red reason,
  no charge, no stuck ghost. Cancel with right-click and Esc, including held click.
- Try F snap off/on and hold Shift for fine placement. Check zoom and all maps,
  especially side-facing doors. P opens/closes the planning footprints.
- Buy three buildings in distinct positions; the fourth must require expansion.
- Meet existing prerequisites, pay120Gold/15Electricity for capacity6. Check the
  room physically grows, furniture/buildings stay put, and new floor accepts builds.
- Check capacities3→6→8→10→11→12→14→16 and no seventeenth purchase.
- Select a machine, Move/V during prep/intermission, commit then cancel another
  move. Check no payment, same level/branch, no cooldown reset or duplicated copy.
- Check moving is unavailable during active combat, but legal buying/upgrades work.
- Try every role, particularly projectile origins/ranges, Dynamo income, repair,
  Decoy targeting, shields and magic defenses after repositioning.
- Upgrade a specialized building to10, move it, inspect it, sell it once: exactly
  50% of actual Gold and Electricity investments, including discounted upgrades.
- Save at a legitimate cleared-wave checkpoint, quit, Continue: exact layout,
  capacity, investments, resources and current spent inventory survive.
- Load a COPY of an older earned checkpoint: no lost machines/levels/branches.
- M still toggles the map; R repairs; Team/spectating/Return to My Room work.
- Compare tightly packed L10 selection comfort with the larger inspector. Report
  confusing hit targets, blocked clicks or unreadable status with a screenshot.
- Run live Endless combat for an extended session. Watch frame pacing, preview
  response, sound balance, node/effect buildup, and reliable cancellation.
- Briefly verify Campaign, Hell/Long Night outside Endless, Practice, Challenge,
  tutorial, saved progression and existing consumables behave as before.

Report the branch/commit, map, difficulty, wave, resolution, graphics/reduced
settings, steps, expected/actual result and screenshot. Do not test corruption
against your original profile; automated corruption tests use isolated copies.
