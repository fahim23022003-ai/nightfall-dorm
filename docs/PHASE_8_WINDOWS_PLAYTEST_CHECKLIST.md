# Phase 8 — owner / Adie Windows playtest

**Native Windows and human approval remain UNVERIFIED.** Use Godot4.6.3, branch
`work`, and back up your normal profile before testing. Do not run developer
fixtures against a real profile; their wallets, levels and starting positions are
prepared inspection states. Normal `project.godot` startup does not use fixtures.

- Launch the existing project. Check cinematic/music, close the welcome card,
  then enter your usual Campaign/Endless or Practice flow.
- At normal listening volume compare the six Hunters' movement, warning and
  release sounds. Check Music/Effects/Ambience and Mute. Watch for overly quiet
  warnings, tiring repetition, harshness or masking by simultaneous turrets.
- In Settings test Low/Medium/High, Reduced Motion, Reduced Flashes and the
  existing Reduced Effects override. Close/relaunch and confirm persistence.
- At1280×720 and1920×1080 inspect six archetypes, especially Ravager/Crusher
  silhouette differences. Compare levels1/4/7/10 without reading the labels.
- Watch walking, stopping, windup, hit, interruption, recoil and defeat.
  Report foot sliding, odd facing or joints that look disconnected.
- Reach a legitimate level10 encounter (Endless wave46 or eligible Campaign
  progression). Observe the once-only reveal and ensure controls remain usable.
  Reaper transformation must still operate separately.
- Defend through evolved/signature warnings. Compare real impacts/HP loss with
  effects. Check Frost/focus interrupts and typed magic defenses. Do not infer
  difficulty approval from prepared screenshot scenarios.
- Build, specialize, upgrade, repair and sell normally. Check actual-spend50%
  refunds, one purchase per intent, cancellation, keyboard shortcuts and resume.
- In a full16-slot room, select each pad and inspect its enlarged preview.
  Check cores/badges/doorHP/telegraphs remain clear under real combat.
- Try each Omen and decline: verify visible state, normal return, rewards and
  checkpoint behavior. Check Team/spectating/Home without editing another room.
- Compare frame pacing during early play, dense combat, transformation and
  High effects. The report's Linux software-rendering numbers predict no
  particular Windows GPU result. Record CPU/GPU/resolution/quality with feedback.

Please separate visual enjoyment, fear/audio preference and actual functional
bugs. Current automated evidence is not a substitute for this human assessment.
