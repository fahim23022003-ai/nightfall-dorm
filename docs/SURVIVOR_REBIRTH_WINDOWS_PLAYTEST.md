# Nightfall Dorm · Nightmare Lobby Windows preview

Extract the archive into an empty folder. Open **Play Nightmare Lobby
Preview.cmd**. It uses the existing Living Nightmare preview profile and
retains Story progress, inventory and preferences. The older Living
Nightmare, V2.3, Procedural V1 and Fixed Reference launchers remain in
the package. Play through one launcher at a time.

- Select **Continue Story**, choose a track and prepare your items. Select
  **Start Match**. Check that the lobby shows the mission and difficulty,
  one human and five AI survivors. Leave Ready untouched once and watch
  the 15-second countdown. Try Ready early and Back on a separate visit.
- Confirm that all six survivors appear together as the match starts.
  Run to a room and press E inside. Try to beat a survivor to an open room.
  A room with an approaching NPC can still be claimed.
- After an ordinary defeat, check the awarded RM Cash and spend it at the
  Shop. Equip an item and confirm it appears on your next Story attempt.
- Replay the tutorial and follow its actual control highlights through
  Needle, Cancel, Dynamo, Upgrade, Repair and closing the panel.
- Compare Standard and Low at 1280×720. Check keyboard focus and a
  960×540 window. Record FPS, GPU, preset, and whether the lobby or combat
  room was displayed.

Windows x64 export verification uses Linux Godot. Native Windows runtime
and Intel UHD FPS require a separate playtest.
