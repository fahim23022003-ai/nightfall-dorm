# Nightfall Dorm · Phase 23 Windows playtest

Extract the ZIP in full and double-click **Play Phase 23 Preview.cmd**. It opens on Low at 960×540. Continue Story uses your existing Phase 22.1 preview profile, including its items, RM Cash and difficulty tracks. Playing either version advances that same preview profile under the usual settlement rules.

- Watch where the Hunter travels when some doors sit behind overlapping firing lanes. A weak door near a heavily armed ally can still be dangerous for the Hunter to approach.
- Try the same chapter from another room. Compare a paid Repair Station with an empty power reserve against one with enough Electricity. Watch for a committed siege, a credible switch, and an approaching threat you can respond to.
- During a Haunted Hall visit, a Decoy can interrupt recovery. Smoke makes your room less attractive when the Hunter next chooses a room.
- Use **Record Phase 23 Diagnostic.cmd** and press **F10** during a busy siege to record frame pacing on your PC. Note the difficulty, mission, room, Hunter, resolution and whether you used Low or Standard.

**Play Phase 22.1 Preview.cmd** starts the prior Story targeting rules. **Play Performance Preview.cmd** starts the earlier performance fallback on its own profile. The Phase 22.1 GitHub download also remains available.
