# Phase 21 Tactical Interface

Extract the Windows x64 ZIP and double-click **Play Phase 21 Preview.cmd**.
The preview starts on Low at 960×540 with a separate profile. For the earlier
layout, open **Play Performance Preview.cmd**. The published Phase 20 build
remains available for comparison with its own saves.

The bottom dock groups **Build**, **Repair**, **Items** and **More**.
Your door meter sits separately. Click the door meter for its armor, upgrades
and instant repair. The **Repair** button and **R** activate continuous repair
with the existing cost and cooldown. Open **More** for the map, camera,
teammates, machine list, Help and Settings.

Click valid floor or choose **Place** on the active placement card. **Q**
rotates and **Esc** closes the card. The same model checks the intended
footprint and access path. **I** opens your equipped items. Inspecting an item
keeps its stock until you explicitly choose **Use selected item**.

**G** opens the pausing guide. **M** opens the map, **H** toggles its danger
shading, and **Home** locks the camera to your own room. Drag with the middle
button to look around. Normal Story, saves and balance follow Phase 20 rules.

The three tutorial lessons teach finding and sleeping in a room, buying a
Dynamo and placing a Needle. Select **Replay Tutorial** from the main menu.
Use **Record Phase 21 Diagnostic.cmd** to gather timing on a separate
temporary profile. Press **F10** at the end to save the report.

Linux Godot verifies the export and UI interactions. Native Windows and Intel
UHD timing require a native playtest.
