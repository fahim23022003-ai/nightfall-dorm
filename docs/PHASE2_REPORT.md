## Implemented

### A. Horror audio

`AudioDirector` uses 37 original, locally stored PCM WAVs generated by the reproducible standard-library script `tools/audio/generate_audio.py`. There are separate 12-second menu/calm/danger music loops, dorm/proximity/generator ambience and distinct action effects. Sources and detailed event routing: [AUDIO_SOURCES.md](AUDIO_SOURCES.md).

Three music stems start once and crossfade smoothly. Actual hunter distance, target/state/level and player-door health raise proximity/danger layers; death/distance reduces them. Combat IDs drive shot and impact cues. Actual attack counters/door HP, construction and upgrade snapshots, resource changes and terminal events drive other effects. UI, purchase/failure and rank/reward events are connected. Ghost defeat and victory are separate sounds.

Music/Effects/Ambient buses, saved individual sliders/mute, per-event cooldowns, distance attenuation, sixteen reusable voices and per-update budgets limit overlap. Critical breach/outcome/escalation/promotion cues reserve capacity, steal ordinary voices or retry through a bounded queue. The three music streams do not restart on every frame or match. Normal Exit/window close saves and drains audio before quitting. Tests also drain playback before node teardown to avoid forced-exit audio-thread warnings.

### B. Permanent ranks and equipped rewards

Six original SVG badges and ranks: Bronze0, Silver250, Gold700, Platinum1400, Diamond2400, Master3800 lifetime XP. Menu rank/XP/next goal, progression bar, rank requirements, unlocked/next rewards, loadout and post-match breakdown are functional. Promotion animates and plays rank/reward sounds. Rewards unlock automatically from rank; Equip changes the next-match slot, and clicking an equipped reward unequips it.

| Rank | Optional reward | Actual effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Pocket change | +5 starting player gold |
| Silver | Reinforced frame | +5% player-door HP, retained through upgrades |
| Gold | Deep sleeper | +3% occupied-bed income |
| Platinum | Clean current | +4% electricity generation |
| Diamond | Steady aim | +3% actual turret projectile damage |
| Master | Last light | Once per match, heal10% max door HP after a nonlethal strike leaves it at/below25%; cannot revive a destroyed door |

One slot initially, configurable in `RankCatalog.MAX_EQUIPPED`. No boost is equipped automatically on a fresh profile. Loadout/difficulty snapshot at match start; changes to future selections cannot alter the active room. Bots do not receive ranked bonuses.

Points require at least20 seconds actually sleeping and match time at least preparation+10s. A win gives120 base; survival gives3 per10 occupied seconds capped90; player-owned actual damage gives1 per50 capped100; an intact surviving door gives1 per50 actual damage sustained capped30. Hard mode adds25% before loss adjustment. Defeats receive35% of those performance points, capped80. The XP ceiling has an explicit adjustment. Results cannot settle twice; abandon/restart grants no reward. These are bounded local progression rules, not server-backed anti-cheat or an online leaderboard.

`user://profile.json` stores versioned XP, equipped rewards, settings, monotonic match IDs, immutable active snapshot and the previous award. Unlocks derive from saved XP. Writes validate a temporary generation, preserve a valid `.bak`, then replace the primary. Corrupt primary data is preserved before recovery/reset; future versions become read-only. Malformed nested IDs/loadouts/chronology, invalid numbers and mismatched result snapshots reject. Failed writes warn and offer a result-screen retry. Maximum-XP saves remain writable.

### C. Gradual hunter escalation and intelligence

Night watch keeps the previous normal first45 active seconds:5000HP,22 damage,1.1s interval,80px/s. Each45-second level warning begins an eight-second smooth ramp toward the next increment (+100max/current HP, +10damage, −0.04s interval). Missing HP is preserved; no refill/resurrection occurs. Movement grows1.5px/s per increment, capped+8; attack interval stays at least0.45s.

Long night is opt-in: ×1.15 initial/growing HP and damage, ×1.05 speed, ×1.25 XP. Values live in catalogs. The HUD and audio warn at actual threat level changes.

Room scoring includes actual door HP/distance plus bounded in-range turret/relay DPS and repair pressure. Retargeting respects commitment and near-breach doors, favors materially better alternatives, and escapes repeated siege windows with insufficient progress. Each retained window refreshes its HP baseline. Recovery changes a stuck movement target without teleporting. Corridor/door/BREACH/EXIT geometry remains intact; closed doors still protect survivors.

## Bugs found and fixed

- Save nested IDs accepted fractional/overflow values; nested locked/unknown/multiple perks and model/snapshot mismatch accepted. Strict schema and snapshot checks now reject them.
- Cross-record match chronology allowed an older result after a newer award; ordering now rejects it, including reload.
- XP at the supported ceiling could no longer save after a win; award clipping and a visible adjustment fix this.
- Corrupt JSON emitted engine errors despite recovery; the parser now returns structured failure without spamming engine errors.
- Critical victory/promotion cues could disappear under voice saturation; prioritization/retry fixes the reproduced case.
- A defense-aware hunter could remain at a repairing door and let passive players be carried. Siege-progress windows restored pressure: final48 matrix has zero passive wins.
- Wrong ownership of `auto_accept_quit` caused startup failure. It belongs to SceneTree; corrected startup and rendered reruns pass.
- Last light description overlapped reward buttons. Explicit short lines and two actual scene bounds assertions fix it; coordinator inspected corrected rendered frames.
- Forced audio teardown left queued playback references. Graceful shutdown and test drain remove the observed warning in corrected paths; abrupt forced engine termination remains a separate lifecycle limitation.

Initial failures are retained as labeled historical evidence; no failing assertion was deleted to manufacture success.
