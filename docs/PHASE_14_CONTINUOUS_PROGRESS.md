The denominator accounts for Bed and Door branch purchases at displayed L5 that immediately advance the structure to L6. Their alternate states are reachable at L6–L10, yielding 20 core variants and 180 placeable-building variants.

Frost uses a static crystal and true firing flash. Shock's raised assembly follows the existing discharge flash. The Amplifier follows occupied production and the Relay shows activity when a room weapon fires.
- Bed has physical woodwork, iron headboard and canopy progression, with Lucid Dividend and Dream Conductor hardware from L6. Several transitions remain subtle. Some other structures also retain modest silhouette changes between adjacent tiers.
