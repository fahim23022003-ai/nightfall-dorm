## Hunter pressure and bot behavior

No enormous HP patch, new full-heal retreat, wallet-triggered scaling or new enemy was added. Existing archetypes, evolution cap10, skills, bounded waves, Reaper and counters remain. A genuine sustained attack within180 units of a valid intact door accumulates contact; actual positive main-Hunter damage counts hits.20s+3hits and40s+6hits each warn for2.5s before an8% damage stack, capped at two per encounter. Fake/blocked hits cannot farm growth. Transformed Reaper/domain do not accumulate additional stacks; existing stacks remain bounded. Deliberate next-wave creation resets encounter pressure while existing progression continues.

V2 damage pacing before mitigation/shields/Legendary rescue: Normal.72→1 over300s, Long Night.78→1.08 over450s, Hell.65→1.05 over600s, multiplied by at most1.16 contact pressure and existing difficulty/skills. Non-Normal contact repair effectiveness has a.65 floor before existing Endless/Nightmare modifiers. Existing target commitment, telegraphs, valid pathing, recovery windows and cooldowns remain. EXIT state is post-elimination travel, **not HP recovery**. No added healing retreat means no new retreat cooldown or full-heal stalemate.

Bots spend real room funds through shared versioned requirements, prioritize essential weapon/bed/door/Dynamo, save for prerequisite investments and use bounded time-dependent goals. They receive no new free resources. The harder-difficulty failures include allies falling early: NPC survival and timing remain part of the human balance review.
